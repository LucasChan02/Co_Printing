The IRB 1200 comes in three variants -7 kg/0.9 m, 7 kg/0.7 m, 5 kg/0.9 m. All IRB 1200 variants can be mounted at any angle and come with IP 40 protection as standard. Options include Food Grade Lubrication, Foundry Plus 2, IP67, and Cleanroom.

The IRB 1200 is powered by [IRC5 controllers](https://new.abb.com/products/robotics/controllers/irc5-overview/irc5).
